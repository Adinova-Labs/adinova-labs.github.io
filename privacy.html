1. Introduction

Adinova Labs ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes mobile applications, websites, and digital services. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, shared, and protected when you use our applications, websites, and services.

This policy is intended to cover our range of applications and services. The information processed may differ depending on the features, permissions, technologies, and third-party services used by a particular application.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the application and the features you use, the following categories of information may be processed:

2.1 Device and Technical Information

Certain applications or integrated services may process technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, language, crash reports, diagnostic information, and general performance data.

2.2 Usage Information

Where analytics or diagnostic tools are implemented, information about application interactions, feature usage, errors, and performance may be collected to understand and improve the service.

2.3 Information You Provide

Some services may allow you to submit information such as your name, email address, messages, preferences, or other details you choose to provide.

The information requested will depend on the specific service. We aim to request only information reasonably necessary for the feature being used.

2.4 Locally Stored Information

Certain applications may store preferences, settings, saved content, or reminder configurations on your device. The availability and type of local storage depend on the particular application.

3. How We Use Information

Where applicable, information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate application features.
  • Remember user preferences and settings.
  • Provide reminders and notifications requested by users.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve our services.
  • Detect technical errors, abuse, or security issues.
  • Respond to support requests and user enquiries.
  • Process purchases or verify access to paid features.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not claim that every application performs all of these activities. Processing depends on the features and technologies actually implemented in that service.

4. Permissions, Notifications and Reminders

Some applications may request device permissions to provide particular features. These may include notification permissions or other permissions relevant to the functionality of the application.

Notifications and reminders may be scheduled locally on your device or delivered through a service, depending on how the application is implemented.

You can manage available notification permissions through your device settings. Disabling a permission may affect the associated feature.

We do not imply that an application accesses contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or other sensitive device resources unless that access is actually implemented and appropriately disclosed.

5. Purchases, Subscriptions and Payments

Certain applications may offer paid features, subscriptions, one-time purchases, trials, or other digital products. Where Google Play Billing is used, purchase transactions are processed through Google Play in accordance with Google's applicable terms and policies.

We may receive or process purchase-related information necessary to verify transactions, determine subscription status, provide purchased features, and handle billing support.

When payments are processed by Google Play, payment card and payment account details are generally handled by Google's payment systems rather than directly by our application. The precise information available to us depends on the billing integration.

Subscription prices, trial eligibility, renewal terms, cancellation procedures, and access periods are disclosed through the applicable purchase interface and the relevant platform.

6. Advertising and Analytics

Some applications may use advertising or analytics services. These services will only apply where they are actually integrated into the relevant application.

Depending on the service and configuration, third-party providers may process device identifiers, diagnostic information, advertising-related information, or usage data under their own privacy policies.

For example, if Google AdMob is integrated into an application, Google's applicable policies and disclosures may also apply.

An application that does not include advertising or analytics integrations does not use those integrations merely because they are mentioned in this policy.

7. Third-Party Services

Our applications or websites may rely on third-party services to provide functionality such as app distribution, payment processing, hosting, diagnostics, analytics, notifications, or customer support.

Those providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services and under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of relevant third-party providers. We do not control the independent privacy practices of services operated by other organizations.

8. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information as a general business practice.

Where applicable, information may be shared with service providers that help operate our services, with platforms that process purchases, when required by law, to address security or fraud concerns, or when necessary to protect legal rights and comply with lawful obligations.

Any sharing depends on the information involved, the service being used, and the applicable legal requirements.

9. Data Storage, Security and Retention

Depending on the application, information may be stored locally on your device or processed by the infrastructure and service providers used by that application.

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the service to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide the service, to meet applicable legal obligations, or to resolve legitimate disputes. Retention periods may differ by information type and service.

Depending on the application and applicable law, you may be able to manage certain information through application settings, device permissions, platform settings, or by contacting us.

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your use of our services or to request deletion where applicable.

Depending on the application and the service providers involved, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country in which you live. Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to ensure that such processing follows relevant legal requirements.
